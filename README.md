# Ashen Covenant

A roguelike pixel-art auto-battler for your phone. Draft a mercenary company, forge items, and march through three acts to face the Ashwalker.

**Play:** https://thewingdevisback.github.io/ashen-covenant/

Open the link in Safari or Chrome and choose *Add to Home Screen* to install it. It works offline after the first visit.

## Assets and licence

Everything in the game is made in code: the characters, items, effects and backgrounds are drawn at runtime on a canvas, and every sound and the music are synthesised live with the Web Audio API (oscillators and filtered noise). There are no image, sound or music files from anywhere else.

The only outside assets are three fonts loaded from Google Fonts: IM Fell English SC, Silkscreen and Barlow Semi Condensed. All three are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows use in any project, free or commercial.
